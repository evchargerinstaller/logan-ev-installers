# EV Charger Installer in Logan, QLD

Logan EV Installers has been in business since 2015 and works as an [EV charger installer in Logan](https://loganevinstallers.com.au), installing home chargers for Logan drivers. With 1500+ EV charger related jobs completed, we know what works for local homes and switchboards.

## Why choose Logan EV Installers

Every job is done by a licensed electrician. We check your switchboard before we quote, give you a fixed upfront price, then test the charger, set up the app and hand over your compliance certificate.

We recommend the right charger for your car and property, from a 7kW single-phase unit to a 22kW three-phase system, based on what your Logan home can support.
